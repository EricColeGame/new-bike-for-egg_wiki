### Step 2 — Confirm What's Available

Reward pools rotate. Before you commit an evening to a grind, check the current state of the shop and any active event. Five minutes of verification beats three hours of wasted effort.

### Step 3 — Buy a Workhorse First

### Step 4 — Grind What You Can Finish

Consistency wins. A route you can complete reliably every time beats a high-risk run you fail half the time. Player experience backs this up — the people with the rarest collections are usually the ones running boring, repeatable loops.

### Step 5 — Close the Gap With Trading

If the mythic tier is only reachable through trading, your buffer becomes leverage. Know roughly what your items are worth before you open a trade window, and don't let excitement push you into an obvious overpay.

### The Three-Question Test

Before buying any new bike for egg currency, ask:

1. **Does this beat what I already own in a stat I actually use?**
2. **Can I earn back the cost in a reasonable number of sessions?**
3. **Will I still want this after the next update?**

Two out of three "yes" is usually enough. One out of three means you're buying a screenshot, not an upgrade.
