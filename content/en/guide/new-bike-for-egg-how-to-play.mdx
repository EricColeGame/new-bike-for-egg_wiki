Roblox simulators come and go, but New Bike for Egg has quietly become one of the most addictive egg-stealing tycoons on the platform — and if you just spawned in with no idea what to tap first, you're not alone. The game blends treadmill training, pet hatching, and base upgrades into a loop where speed decides who leaves with the loot. This guide walks through exactly how to play New Bike for Egg, from your very first egg to your first Secret-rarity hatch.

## What New Bike for Egg Is All About

Bike For Egg is listed on Roblox as a simulation and tycoon experience, and that label fits: you're building a base, farming passive income, and pushing one stat higher and higher. The twist is that your income comes from pets, and your pets come from eggs — eggs you hatch yourself or swipe from other players.

The [official Bike For Egg page on Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/games/74023858073178/Bike-For-Egg) keeps the loop short: steal eggs from pets, hatch them, earn money from what hatches, upgrade your treadmill and base, train for Speed, and repeat with rarer eggs, bigger pets, and mutations. Two of the game's badges are tied to hatching an Eternal-rarity pet and a Secret-rarity pet, which tells you exactly where the long game is pointing.

| System | What you actually do | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Egg stealing | Grab eggs from pets and from other players | Fastest early source of new pets |
| Hatching | Crack eggs to collect pets | Pets are your income engine |
| Pet income | Earn money passively from your pets | Funds every upgrade you'll buy |
| Treadmill | Train to build Speed | Speed wins chases and shortens runs |
| Base upgrades | Upgrade treadmill and base | Raises your overall ceiling |
| Animal Index | Fill out the index | One-time rewards for completion |

## New Bike for Egg: How to Play, Step by Step

If you remember one thing about how to play New Bike for Egg, make it this: speed comes first. Every other system — stealing, hatching, upgrading — gets easier once your character can outrun the map and outpace other players.

1. **Claim the freebie first.** Joining hands you a welcome badge, and the game page nudges you to like the experience and join the group for rewards and update news. Do both before you start grinding.
2. **Train on the treadmill immediately.** Speed is the stat that unlocks everything else, so resist the urge to wander the map for twenty minutes.
3. **Collect your first eggs.** Start with eggs near pets, which are far safer than contesting another player on day one.
4. **Hatch right away.** An unhatched egg earns you nothing; a hatched pet starts paying out immediately.
5. **Equip your highest earners.** Swap in the pets that generate the most money rather than the ones that look coolest.
6. **Upgrade the treadmill, then the base.** Alternate between the two so your Speed and your income grow together.
7. **Start stealing from players.** Once your Speed is respectable, player eggs become the better prize.
8. **Chase the Animal Index.** New species, sizes, and mutations all feed the index, and the index pays rewards.

| Session stage | Main action | Target |
|---|---|---|
| First 5 minutes | Treadmill training | Get moving faster than default |
| 5–15 minutes | Egg collection and hatching | Fill your first pet slots |
| 15–30 minutes | Equip best pets, buy upgrades | Steady passive income |
| 30+ minutes | Player stealing and index hunting | Rarer eggs and pets |

## Speed, Your Bike, and the Right Upgrade Order

The bike is the identity of the game, and in practice your bike and your Speed stat are part of the same conversation. Faster training means faster runs, and faster runs mean you actually reach the egg instead of watching it vanish. Community reports consistently rank treadmill levels above cosmetic spending in the early game, simply because Speed compounds: every upgrade makes the next egg run shorter.

A simple rule that works in most tycoon simulators applies here too — buy the upgrade that makes money faster before you buy the one that makes you look better. In New Bike for Egg, that means treadmill first, base second, everything else after.

| Priority | Upgrade | Reasoning | When to switch |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Treadmill levels | Speed gates stealing, travel, and survival | Keep topping up all session |
| 2 | Base upgrades | Raises your income ceiling | After every few treadmill levels |
| 3 | Better eggs | Higher-tier eggs mean better pets | Once income feels steady |
| 4 | Pet slot optimization | More earning pets online at once | When a new pet beats an equipped one |
| 5 | Cosmetic or flex purchases | No gameplay benefit | Last, always |

## Egg Stealing: The Strategy Most Players Miss

Stealing is where new players lose the most progress, usually because they pick fights they can't win. Player experience suggests a few habits separate steady earners from players who keep resetting.

- **Farm pets before players.** Pet-held eggs are uncontested and let you build Speed and income without risk.
- **Scout before you sprint.** Watch a target's movement for a few seconds — a slow player is a safe steal, a fast one is a trap.
- **Bank your wins.** Hatch what you steal quickly so a bad run doesn't cost you everything.
- **Steal up, not sideways.** Target eggs from tiers above your current hatch level instead of duplicates you'll never use.
- **Retreat on cooldown.** If a chase turns into a marathon, break off and train; there's always another egg.

Bike For Egg doesn't publish official hatch odds, so the tier list below reflects community reports plus the two rarities the game itself confirms through badges.

| Tier | Typical source | What to expect |
|---|---|---|
| Common / Uncommon | Starter eggs and easy steals | Fast hatches; use as early cash, replace quickly |
| Rare | Mid-tier eggs | First real jump in pet income |
| Epic / Legendary | Higher-cost eggs, lucky steals | Worth saving eggs and money for |
| Eternal | Rare hatches (confirmed by an in-game badge) | A long-term goal, not a first-hour result |
| Secret | Rarest confirmed badge tier | Chase once your base and Speed are solid |

## Pets, Money, and the Animal Index

Pets are the engine of New Bike for Egg, and the Animal Index is the receipt. Every new species, size, and mutation you discover feeds the index, and completing entries pays out rewards you can put straight back into upgrades. That makes hatching variety genuinely profitable rather than just a collection hobby.

| Goal | How to work toward it | Payoff |
|---|---|---|
| Higher passive income | Equip top-earning pets, hatch better eggs | Faster upgrades across the board |
| Full Animal Index | Hatch new species instead of duplicates | Claimable index rewards |
| Bigger and mutated pets | Keep hatching at higher tiers | Higher-value pets and rarer entries |
| Rare badges | Target Eternal and Secret hatches | Permanent account flex |

Treat the index as a checklist, not a side quest. When you're deciding whether to spend on another treadmill level or a fresh egg, check which one moves an unfinished index entry forward — that's usually the better value.

## FAQ

**How do you get started in New Bike for Egg?**
Train on the treadmill first, then collect and hatch eggs from pets before you try stealing from other players. Speed and early pets make every later step easier, and both are available within your first few minutes.

**Can you play New Bike for Egg solo?**
Yes, but you'll progress slower. Pet-held eggs are solo-friendly, while stealing from other players is the faster route once your Speed is competitive.

**How rare are Eternal and Secret pets?**
Both are confirmed by in-game badges, and community reports describe them as long-term goals rather than quick wins. Keep hatching at the highest tier you can afford and treat them as bonuses, not targets.

**Are there New Bike for Egg codes for free rewards?**
No verified codes were available at the time of writing. The game page and the official group are the only reliable places to check for reward announcements and updates.

**What's the fastest way to grow in New Bike for Egg?**
Alternate treadmill and base upgrades, hatch constantly, and steal only when you know you're faster than your target. Steady compounding beats one lucky gamble almost every session.
