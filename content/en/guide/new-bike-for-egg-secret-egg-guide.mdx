### Don't Skip the Index

Every new pet you hatch typically unlocks an index reward — cash, speed, or both, according to community reports. New players skip this system entirely and then wonder why they're broke at level ten. Clear early index entries before you spend a single egg on a bike, because those rewards are effectively free funding for the purchase.

If you feel stuck, check the index before you check the shop. There is almost always an unclaimed entry sitting there.

## Night Cycles, Resets, and Offline Egg Income

Two systems quietly do most of the heavy lifting in the background: the night cycle and offline earnings. Eggs can grow dramatically faster during night hours — community reports put the multiplier high enough that overnight timers become realistic instead of punishing. Place your best eggs right before night hits, and never waste the multiplier on cheap filler eggs.

| Egg type | Night priority | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Secret eggs | Very high | Long timers shrink overnight |
| Huge rare eggs | High | Expensive to rush any other way |
| Index eggs | Medium | Free rewards at low cost |
| Small normal eggs | Low | Already fast |

Egg resets matter just as much. Every reset is a fresh chance at a secret or an oversized egg, so don't sit idle between them. Sweep your unlocked areas, grab anything huge, then get back to hatching. Players who treat resets as background noise fall behind players who treat them as appointments.

Offline income is the third pillar. Your equipped pets keep earning while you're away, so a short login — claim, buy one upgrade, scan for obvious big eggs, log off — keeps the loop moving even on a busy day.

## FAQ

### How many eggs should I save before buying a new bike?

Buy as soon as travel time is the thing slowing you down, not when you hit an arbitrary number. Keep enough of your best pets equipped to fund the purchase, and never sell off your income engine to afford a bike. A modest bike plus strong pets beats an expensive bike with nothing behind it.

### Is this new bike for egg secret egg guide useful for complete beginners?

Yes — beginners gain the most from it. New players who clear the index, equip their biggest eggs, and buy travel speed early tend to pull ahead of players who chase rare eggs while broke. The order of operations matters more than raw playtime.

### Do I need the most expensive bike in the shop?

No. The entry tier usually covers every zone you can realistically reach at your stage. Upgrade later, once your pets can pay for the next model without wiping out your egg bank.

### Why do faster players always get the secret eggs?

Because secret spawns are races, not lotteries. The player who arrives first gets the hatch. If you keep losing spawns, your speed is the problem — not your luck. Fix speed first, then worry about pet quality.
