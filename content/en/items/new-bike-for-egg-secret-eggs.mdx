## What "New Bike for Egg" Really Means in Roblox

Every session in Bike For Egg comes down to one decision: spend your cash now or keep saving for a new bike for egg. Bike For Egg is a collection-and-speed game. You spawn on a bike, ride around the map, and scoop up eggs. Those eggs convert into Money, and Money is the only thing standing between you and your next ride. The loop is simple, but the pacing isn't: the starter bike is slow, egg spawns are spread across the map, and the top-tier bike costs more than most casual players will ever bank.

That gap is exactly why so many players search for a shortcut. The short answer is that there are only three bikes, so the real skill isn't picking the right one — it's earning the money to buy it quickly. The longer answer is below.

The Standard Bike is your starting ride and the x1 speed baseline everything else is measured against. Don't rush to replace it. Spend your first few sessions learning where eggs cluster, how long they take to respawn, and which corners of the map are worth the detour. That map knowledge is worth more than a small speed boost.

This is the purchase that changes how the game feels. At 200 Money, it's cheap enough to earn in a single focused session, and the speed increase makes every future egg run more efficient. The 1 Robux alternative exists for players who would rather skip the grind, but 200 Money is genuinely reachable — treat the Robux option as a convenience, not a necessity.

Save for it after the Football Bike, once you can run a loop without thinking about it.
