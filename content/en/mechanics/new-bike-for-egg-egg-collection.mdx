The upgrade only pays off if your route is built for it. Riders who keep doing single-zone loops on a fast bike get almost none of the benefit — they're just finishing the same short trip sooner. The real gain comes from expanding the loop.

Three habits separate efficient riders from busy ones:

- **Skip the scraps.** Low-value eggs near the hub aren't worth a trip once you can reach better zones. Speed creates the option to be picky.
- **Batch your shop visits.** Every return to base should accomplish something — sell, hatch, upgrade, then head back out.
