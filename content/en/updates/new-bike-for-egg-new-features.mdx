## Best Ways to Use the New Bike for Egg

Owning the bike is the easy part. Using it well is where the skill gap appears.

| Situation | Recommended Approach | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Long open routes | Hold cruising speed, avoid weaving | Constant corrections bleed time |
| Clustered spawns | Dismount between nearby eggs | Remounting costs less than slow navigation |

### Route Planning Beats Reflexes

The biggest gains come from redrawing your route, not from riding better. Sketch a loop that hits three or four clusters in sequence, then time it. Most players find their first route is too ambitious and their second is too conservative — the third is usually right.
