## Riding Tips That Actually Save You Time

The single biggest gain from the **new bike for egg new update** is not raw speed, it is route compression. A player who knows where every egg spawns and rides a clean loop will beat a faster rider who wanders. Build the route first, then add speed.

- **Park facing your exit.** Dismount pointing toward your next egg, not back the way you came.
- **Chain spawns clockwise or counterclockwise.** Never zigzag across the middle of the map.
- **Save the boost for gaps, not straightaways.** Boost is your escape tool, not your cruise control.
- **Learn the summon cooldown.** If you get separated from the bike, you need to know how long you are stuck walking.
- **Test collision on ramps.** Community reports frequently mention clipping on steep slopes, so ride them once in a safe lobby.
- **Upgrade handling before top speed.** Twitchy steering costs more time than a slightly lower top speed.

| Route type | Time saved vs. walking | Risk level | Best use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Full map perimeter loop | High | Low | Farming known spawn points |
| Hub-to-outskirts sprint | Moderate | Low | Grabbing one contested egg |
| Indoor multi-floor building | Negative | High | Skip the bike entirely |
| Chase escape route | High | Moderate | Surviving a hostile lobby |

Also worth doing: set a personal benchmark. Run your usual route on foot, note the time, then run it on the bike. That single comparison tells you more about whether the mount suits your playstyle than any tier list. Benchmarks also help you notice when a later patch quietly changes the handling.

## FAQ

**Is the new bike for egg new update free to use once unlocked?**
In most builds, unlocking is a one-time requirement and riding afterward costs nothing. Some variants and upgrades may require additional currency, so read the requirement text before spending.

**Does the bike make egg hunting easier for beginners?**
It helps with distance but punishes imprecision. Beginners should learn the map on foot first, then switch to the bike once they know where the spawns are.

**Why does my bike keep getting stuck on ramps?**
Collision handling on slopes is the most frequently reported issue from player experience. Approach ramps straight on, reduce speed before the incline, and report consistent failures with a clip.

**Should I upgrade speed or handling first?**
Handling, almost always. A controllable bike on a clean route beats a fast bike you cannot steer, and handling upgrades make tight sections far less punishing.
